Findings on the baseline (measurements-*.json in before/)
- 667x375 without keyboard: the panel top is 38px (not negative); the old 224px floor was NOT reproduced there
  (composer top 266). It reproduces as soon as the visible height shrinks: panel top -87 (250 high),
  -137 (200), -151 (170); 닫기 is then off screen.
- 667x375 preview: 입력창에 넣기 (top 244, bottom 280) is clipped by the panel bottom (262) and fails the hit test.
- Button row: 단축어 margin-top -8px, 추천 margin-top -6px, gap to composer frame -6px (390) / -2.25px (320) / 2px (1280);
  heights 36 vs 32 (mobile), 28 vs 19.75 (desktop); font 12px vs 11px.

After (measurements-*.json in after/)
- Panel inside the visible viewport in every state; 닫기/입력창에 넣기/저장/취소 inside the viewport and hit-testable.
- Button row: margins 0, gap 6px (390) / 8.62px (320) / 6px (1280), equal heights (36 mobile, 28 desktop), equal top, font 12px, text baselines equal.
- Known limit: at 170-200px visible height the form body shows about one field row; save/cancel/close stay pinned and the browser scrolls a focused field into view.
