## Public surfaces — actual site dataflow
1. **Tagline**: at most 50 Unicode characters, emotionally magnetic, high-stakes character-specific quote or conflict hook. Should pose an immediate question rather than offer a generic 'cold duke' description. Avoid guaranteed future love, immutable user identity or spoilers.
2. **Visible detailed public introduction** (`draft.description`, **at most 3,000 characters**): short canonical-world setup **first**, then clear, appealing character card (name, age, height, **weight only when explicitly approved/established**, attractive identifying features, concise personality/behavior, distinctive weaknesses), then current conflict/hook and possibilities for the player's choice. Plain readable Korean, scan-friendly headings, no repetitive lore encyclopedia, no false identity or relationship locks. This is **not** the 200–500 character short discovery pitch in `bible.publicProfile.description`. The canonical writer currently lives in `src/lib/officialSupply/publicProfileText.ts` via `compileOfficialDraftFromBible` in `bible.ts`; reconcile actual rendering at that owner rather than adding a second public-copy writer or directly editing a generated report.
3. **Greeting / first scene**: natural Korean literary scene **averaging approximately 1,500 characters**; a useful editorial target is about 1,350–1,650 characters, within the existing 2,000-character storage ceiling. This is a **quality target, not a new global hard runtime output limit**. The author-quality band was updated in #1502 to 700–1,800 to accept ~1,500-char greetings while retaining older valid 700–1,400 openings. Keep the **existing canonical author-quality owner**, 2,000-char storage ceiling and Main RP output settings; no added override.
4. **Player agency and neutrality**: a woman, man, nonbinary or unspecified person must be able to inhabit the same opening. The player controls gender, age/persona, occupation, clothing, appearance, prior bonds, movements, spoken words, bodily reactions, emotions, and decisions. The character's existing location and scene premise can be set; descriptions of the player's identity/actions/feelings may not be fabricated. A single scene is used; no forced alternate-episode openings. Offer a meaningful choice of responses without impersonating the player.
5. **Character bible**: preserve identity/spec → behavior-driven personality → 2–3 formative events and their present consequences → user perception/relationship progression → distinctive speech → capabilities with limits and costs → motives/non-negotiables → habits/hobbies, with gender-neutral player reference. Other character relationships belong in canonical lorebook unless directly consequential; no unconditional cast dump in core. Shared-world canon remains one canonical owner; keep exact source/injection boundaries.
6. **Market positioning**: use current, verifiable Korean audience and competitor research **without copying IP**, and retain the existing committed market snapshot as evidence of prior planning. Strong opening tension, competent but fallible love interest, distinct behavioral hooks, optional relationship progression, and emotional stakes are editorial hypotheses to validate, **not claims that every female reader likes the same thing**. GPT is responsible for content assessment.
7. **Honesty and versioning**: proposals not found in existing canon (e.g. a numeric body weight or a forged imperial order) must be marked **NEW EDITORIAL PROPOSAL** until approved. Record approved manuscript/version and preserve the old content for diff; no silent retroactive canon rewrite. A reviewer packet is not a publish approval.
